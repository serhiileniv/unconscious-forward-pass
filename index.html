what this is

A real 12-layer transformer runs in your browser. Attention, superposition, sparsity and suppression are genuinely computed — the geometry you see is the model's own. Two caveats, stated plainly: the network is untrained, so its word choice is anchored by corpus bigram statistics rather than learned language; and the feature dictionary is constructed rather than learned by a sparse autoencoder. This is the architecture of thinking, not a scan of any deployed model.
