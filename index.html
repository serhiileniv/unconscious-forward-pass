what this is

The real published weights, running a real forward pass in your browser. Every token in the vocabulary is drawn, with no subset and no cap on how many may light at once, and placed by a spectral embedding of the model's own nearest-neighbour graph — so weekdays sit with weekdays, digits with digits, cities with cities.

A point lights when that layer pushed the model toward saying that word. The residual stream is a running sum and the output head is linear, so each layer's contribution to every word's score is exactly computable, and the contributions add up to the model's real output. Warm means pushed toward; violet means pushed away.

This is a decomposition, not an estimate. The running total at the final layer equals the model's own logits to the last bit — max absolute error 0.0, checked in tools/evalmodel.ts. An earlier technique, the logit lens, re-normalises at each depth instead; measured on this model it disagrees with the real output at every layer before 20, which is why it is not what you are looking at.

What this does not show: why a layer pushed as it did, or any state the output head cannot see. These are not the words in the model's mind. They are the exact amount each layer moved every word's score, summing to what it actually said.
