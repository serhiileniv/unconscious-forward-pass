[1/6]
I asked Claude to show me its unconscious in 3D.

It said it has no introspective access. Then it pointed at something real: in one forward pass a model moves every word's score, over and over, and one word comes out.

So we built it. Qwen2.5 0.5B, real weights, in the browser.

[2/6]
Every point is a real token, sitting at its own row of the model's output head. Two points are close when the model puts those two words close.

A point lights when that layer pushed the model toward saying it. Warm = pushed toward. Violet = pushed away.

[3/6]
This is not a logit lens. It's an exact decomposition.

The residual stream is a running sum and the output head is linear, so each layer's contribution to every word's score is exactly computable, and the parts sum to the model's real logits.

Max abs error: 0.00e+0

[4/6]
"The Eiffel Tower is located in the city of" → " Paris" (54.4%)

Contribution to " Paris":
L19 +0.65
L20 +5.73
L21 +10.90
L22 −3.86

Layer 21 fires " French" " France" " Paris" " Louis" at once. Layer 22 then pushes " Paris" back down.

[5/6]
Why not the logit lens: on this model it disagrees with the real output at every layer before 20 of 24. KL 8–13, zero top-10 overlap, top tokens " rain", " sequ", " swe".

It would have drawn twenty layers of noise as though it were thought.

[6/6]
What it does not show: why a layer pushed as it did, or anything the output head can't see.

These aren't "the words in its mind". They're the exact amount each layer moved every word's score.
